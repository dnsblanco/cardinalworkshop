Cardinal Workshop was a furniture company co-founded by Bryce Williamson and Dennis Blanco in 2015. We made CNC-milled solid wood furniture at our workshop in Queens, New York and delivered it to customers across the United States.

In 2018, we moved the shop to North Carolina, wound down the brand, and shifted focus to custom manufacturing projects through Bryce's company, Stokes. You can shop Stokes Furniture today.
